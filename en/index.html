FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Did not find an answer? Write to me and we will discuss your situation personally.

How do lessons work?

We meet online in an agreed video service. We use interactive materials, speak, listen, and apply new skills right away.

Can I start from zero?

Yes. The plan and pace depend on your starting level, goals, and the time you are ready to give learning.

How often should I study?

One or two lessons a week is usually a comfortable rhythm. We will decide what suits you after discussing your goal.

Are there homework tasks?

Yes, if agreed. It is short, manageable practice that reinforces the material and helps you move faster.

How do I book a lesson?

Click “Write on Telegram” and briefly tell me who the lessons are for and your main goal.
