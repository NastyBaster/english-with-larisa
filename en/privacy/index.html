Privacy policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. What data is processed

This website does not collect personal data through its own forms or create user accounts. If you follow a messenger link or send an email, your data is processed by the relevant service according to its own rules.

2. How messages are used

Information you voluntarily send is used only to respond to your request, arrange lessons, and communicate about learning.

3. Third-party services

The website may contain links to Telegram or another messenger. After you follow a link, the privacy policy of that service applies.

4. Cookies and analytics

The basic version of this website does not use analytics, advertising pixels, or its own cookies. If these tools are added later, this policy must be updated.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or to request deletion of data you have provided, email laralemishko@gmail.com.
